Selected findings

What the picture supports.

Source timecodes refer to the final film. Frame notation uses 24 fps; stills retain the finished grade.

One cut changes the relationship.

CUT AT 05:36:16

ObservationThe cut lands during the rise to standing. The next angle places both characters side by side; a hand on a back becomes visible.

InterpretationThe movement carries the transition, while the new composition suggests closeness. This makes the edit a useful example of action continuity and relational staging.

03:01–03:32STAGING / A VISUAL TRADE-OFF

Same frame, new occupant.

The son leaves; the father enters the same doorway. Keeping the frame connects them through a shared space.

Trade-off: the son's face has little separation from the shadows. A targeted lift could be tested without flattening the whole scene; the final master limits recovery.

02:48–02:54FOCUS / LEGIBILITY RISK

A discovery kept in the background.

Once the shot settles on the father, the background interaction remains soft. This may make his discovery harder to read on a first viewing.

Next check: can a first-time viewer identify what he notices? A focus pull or an insert would be a shooting revision, not detail recoverable from this master.

07:59–08:12MOTION / ACTION CLARITY

Energy competes with information.

The rapid rotation and motion blur make the decisive physical action difficult to track.

Revision: shorten the rotation and reach the aftermath earlier. A stable view of the protective action would require additional coverage.

Connection to AI video review

Observe. Locate. Explain.

A review format demonstrated on live-action footage, with the same evidence discipline needed for evaluating generated video.

01 / OBSERVE

Describe what is visible.

Separate subject movement, camera movement and edit boundaries. Avoid filling gaps with assumptions about intent or production.

02 / LOCATE

Make the finding reproducible.

Give a source interval and the affected action. A reviewer should be able to find the same evidence and challenge the conclusion.

03 / EXPLAIN

Judge the effect and the limits.

Connect the observation to clarity or continuity. State uncertainty, and distinguish an available edit from a change requiring new footage.

Case study method, AI assistance and source limits

The source is the final 2022 master: 8-bit video with burned-in English subtitles. The camera originals and Resolve project are unavailable. Stills and excerpts retain the finished grade; there is no grading before/after comparison.

AI-assisted tools were used for frame sampling, measurements, analysis drafts, reel assembly and layout. Findings are limited to visible evidence and measured signals. Audio routing and listening-based judgements have not been verified; no claim of a completed listening review is made.

This is a retrospective analysis of a live-action film. The full film, the case study and the analysis reel serve different purposes; source timecodes always refer to the original master.
